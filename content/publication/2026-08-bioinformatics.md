abstract = "Motivation: Hi-C is a powerful technology for mapping chromatin interactions genome-wide. However, interaction loops identified from Hi-C contact maps often vary across replicate experiments due to experimental noise, making reproducibility assessment essential. A major challenge lies in the genomic distance dependence of interaction strength, which systematically affects reproducibility but is overlooked by existing methods for reproducibility assessment. Results: We introduce Stratum-Adjusted Irreproducible Discovery Rate (SIDR), a novel statistical model that integrates distance stratification into the widely-used Irreproducible Discovery Rate (IDR) framework. SIDR explicitly models the confounding effect of genomic distance, enabling global control of irreproducibility across interaction ranges. Through simulations and real Hi-C datasets, we demonstrate that SIDR improves discriminative power and recovers more biologically meaningful interactions than existing approaches, making it a valuable tool for robust and reproducible Hi-C analysis. Availability: The R package SIDR is freely available on GitHub https://github.com/qunhualilab/SIDR."
